# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person who wants to keep their business mental math sharp, drilling case math of the kind consulting screening tests (McKinsey, BCG, Bain) are built from. They practise in short bursts on a phone held in one hand, often standing or between other things, thumb only. Every visit is a few minutes: open, answer, see the result, leave.

## Product Purpose

Margins makes case math fast and reliable through daily practice: typed drills, multiple-choice scenario and chart questions, sprints and timed mocks, each followed by the worked solution and the fastest shortcut. Success is a mock score of 70% or more and a falling average time per drill. The game layer (streak, XP, consulting ranks, mastery stars, badges) exists to bring the owner back every day.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, loud about numbers, a little playful. A match-day scoreboard for an adult: gamified but not childish. The voice is a sharp colleague: short, exact, a little dry ("Clean sheet.", "Worth another go."). Wins flash up on the board and feel earned; misses are named plainly and never punished. Works in the dark and in daylight: a dark theme and a light theme of equal standing, following the phone unless the owner pins one.

## Anti-references

- Cartoon gamification: mascots, confetti storms, cheering copy, rounded candy buttons, hearts and lives.
- SaaS dashboard templates: hero-metric cards, gradient accents, soft shadows, card grids.
- Test-prep sites: dense blue forms, timers in red, exam anxiety.
- Generic dark mode: purple or blue neon, glassmorphism, glow; mono-everything developer-tool looks.

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers are the hero.** The prompt, the answer, the score and the streak are the largest things on screen, in the numeral face. The live number is lit on the board.
2. **One thing to do.** Each screen has one lit action, in reach of the thumb.
3. **Every miss teaches.** Feedback leads with the right answer, then the method and the shortcut. Red marks the miss, nothing more.
4. **Quiet until it counts.** Motion and amber are spent on state changes and real milestones, never on decoration.
5. **Earned familiarity.** Same parts, same places, every screen and both themes; the owner never relearns the interface.
6. **The phone is the device.** Designed for an iPhone 14 Pro held in one hand; nothing important needs a second hand or precise tapping.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text and meaningful graphics), touch targets at least 44px (keys and primary actions larger), full keyboard use on desktop, visible focus. Decorative motion respects reduced motion. Right and wrong are never shown by colour alone (strike-through, labels, stamps).
